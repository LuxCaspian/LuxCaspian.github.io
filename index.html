Teaching machines to learn from data, with curiosity and precision

I'm Fathir Ade Deo, a university student focused on Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, taking on freelance projects under the name Croissant.AI.

I like understanding technology down to its fundamentals. That's why I built a small GPT-style language model from scratch instead of just calling a ready-made library. I also built a mood-based music recommendation system using Spotify data.

All of my work is open on GitHub: the code can be read, run, and inspected directly.
